# ADR-0510: Premium And Microphone Entitlement Behavior Verification

Status: Accepted

## Context

AI Tutor and microphone practice can create provider costs, privacy obligations, and school-policy requirements. They must remain optional white-label capabilities rather than hidden assumptions in the core student journey.

## Decision

Execute microphone and premium AI Tutor entitlement behavior in the compiled foundation behavior harness.

The harness covers:

- review-only microphone activation rejection;
- complete premium AI Tutor validation;
- review-only no-side-effect behavior for a complete premium request.

## Consequences

- The platform can validate a premium package without dispatching a provider.
- Microphone and AI Tutor remain teacher/school controlled and cost-visible.
- Core progression remains independent of both features.
