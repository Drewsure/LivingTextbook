# ADR 0125: Publisher Maintenance Change Queue

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The publisher maintenance panel already described domains, release windows, standing rules, and guardrails. It needed a more operational shape for year-on-year updates.

## Decision

Add a sample maintenance change queue covering media replacement, game availability, and QR alias update requests.

## Consequences

- Partner maintenance becomes easier to explain in product demos.
- Future admin editing has a clearer record shape.
- Media, game, route, and report impacts stay reviewed together.
- Production mutation remains deferred until backend, policy, and approval rules exist.
