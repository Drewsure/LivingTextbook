# ADR 1438: Tenant Pilot Stage Rail

## Status

Accepted for the first saleable white-label pilot.

## Decision

Every non-reference tenant pilot dashboard presents the same tenant-generic
stage rail: publisher intake, content/media review, curated package, QR
release, delivery decision, and teacher-led launch. The rail is a review-only
orientation surface and carries no sample publisher evidence or live write
controls.

## Consequences

Publishers and schools can see the complete path without inheriting MiniStar
data or being promised classroom readiness prematurely. Each stage remains
closed until its own evidence, policy, release, and human decision gates are
complete.
