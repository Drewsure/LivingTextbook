# 0255 School Policy Text Version Pack

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-07-16

## Context

The school policy acceptance preflight identifies missing policy text and scope. Before a school can ever accept terms, the exact policy clauses must be reviewable, versioned, and clearly separate from live acceptance.

## Decision

Add a review-only `School policy text version pack` derived from the school policy acceptance preflight.

The pack names required policy text for privacy, learner data, QR/progression rules, publisher media and local package responsibilities, microphone and AI Tutor optional features, hosted/local storage, rollback, evidence, signature method, and revocation.

## Consequences

- School, publisher, and platform review can see the exact clause areas before acceptance workflow design.
- Future acceptance work has a clear policy text dependency instead of relying on meeting notes.
- No policy acceptance, accept button, signed approval capture, evidence export, storage activation, launch-ready status, production QR promise, AI Tutor activation, real learner data collection, report export, or live classroom workflow is enabled.
