# 0248 School Launch Policy Gate

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-07-16

## Context

The classroom launch gate blocks live student sessions, but a white-label partner still needs a clearer separation between a polished controlled demo and a school-approved classroom launch. Schools own privacy, retention, reporting, access control, learner-data use, support-language policy, microphone policy, AI Tutor adoption, and local deployment acceptance. Publishers own media rights, replacement policy, and local package asset acceptance. The platform owns storage, evidence, release-control, active route, and launch safety gates.

## Decision

Add a `School launch policy gate preview` on the teacher intake page and focused classroom launch gate route.

The gate is derived from pilot policy readiness, the classroom launch gate, and teacher dry-run rehearsal evidence. It shows ownership lanes for school privacy/retention, classroom operating mode, publisher media/local package acceptance, teacher dry-run evidence, and platform release/storage acceptance.

## Consequences

- Partner demos can be confidently shown without implying a school-approved live launch.
- School-owned policy obligations stay visible before real learner data, report export, or production student accounts exist.
- Publisher-owned media and local package obligations stay visible before local/closed deployment is promised.
- No policy acceptance workflow, approval workflow, live launch button, release mutation, evidence export, report export, local activation, or learner-data collection is enabled by this slice.
