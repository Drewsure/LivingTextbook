# DR-554: Partner Pilot Meeting Agenda

Status: Accepted

## Decision

The partner pilot requirements intake must include a first partner pilot meeting agenda. The agenda shows the sequence of topics, questions to ask, evidence to request, decisions not made during the meeting, and live actions still blocked.

## Rationale

White-label pilots need a repeatable adult-facing first conversation. The agenda keeps a publisher or school focused on source package, multimedia rights, activity pathway, QR/front-door entry, learner data policy, reporting, deployment, optional premium AI, and outside prototype timing without implying that the platform is already accepting files or launching real classes.

## Guardrails

- No file collection.
- No partner answer capture.
- No policy acceptance.
- No storage vendor selection.
- No report export promise.
- No local app or offline-ready promise.
- No premium AI Tutor adoption.
- No microphone prompt.
- No Z.ai source handoff request.
- No classroom launch.
