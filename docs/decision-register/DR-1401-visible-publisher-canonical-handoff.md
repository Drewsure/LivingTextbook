# DR-1401: Visible Publisher Canonical Handoff

## Decision

Show the intake-to-canonical-source-manifest bridge in the tenant-scoped
publisher pilot requirements workspace.

## Boundary

The panel is instructional and review-only. It does not execute the command,
write or upload content, assemble packages, print QR codes, activate
persistence, or enable students.

## Verification

Typecheck, foundation composition, and the active route verifier cover the
panel's command, next-gate, and blocked-action markers.
