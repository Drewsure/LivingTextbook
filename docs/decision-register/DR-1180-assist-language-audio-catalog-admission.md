# DR-1180: Assist-Language Audio Catalog Admission Evidence

Decision: expose a review-only, provider-neutral catalog admission record for
each support-language audio gloss.

Required evidence includes checksum, transcript, spoken-text match, rights,
accessibility fallback, source lineage, and hosted/local delivery references.
The sample remains blocked because its support-language cue and audio asset are
not bound. No upload, storage write, approval, promotion, local activation,
student-facing use, speech billing, or support-language progression is enabled.
