# DR-019: White-Label Pilot Readiness Surface

Status: Accepted

Decision: Add a white-label pilot readiness surface and partner pilot timeline so external conversations use a disciplined product promise instead of a vague demo claim.

White-label impact: Strongly positive. The platform now visibly separates MiniStar proof work from the broader saleable product path.

Cost impact: Positive. A narrow 8-12 week pilot promise avoids overbuilding all 48 modes, AI Tutor, persistence, full offline packaging, and automated PDF import before partner requirements are confirmed.

Component boundary: Positive. Pilot readiness is data-driven and rendered through a reusable panel rather than hidden in prose or MiniStar-only screens.

Constraints:

- Do not call the current foundation a finished product.
- Do not promise all games, AI Tutor, automated PDF import, or local packaging in the first pilot.
- First partner pilot scope should stay narrow: one tenant, two to four units, two to four game modes, one multimedia package, and one teacher report view.
- A second sample tenant config should be added before a serious external demo.
