# DR-1123: Browser, Privacy, and Tenant Evidence Packet

## Decision

Keep browser continuity, privacy-negative, and tenant-isolation evidence in a
single exact-scope review packet before any pilot decision can rely on it.

## Status

Implemented and verified as review-only evidence. The sample packet remains
pending until an adult or approved browser automation run captures the lanes.

## Guardrail

Pending evidence must remain visibly pending. The packet cannot authorize
hosted persistence, learner-data collection, export, promotion, classroom
launch, or QR mutation.
