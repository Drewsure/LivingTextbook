# DR-1181: Assist-Language Audio Catalog Approval Boundary

Decision: add a tenant-scoped, review-only approval packet for support-audio
catalog admission.

The packet names the future teacher or publisher audio owner, required and
unresolved evidence, next records, and a `not-recorded` decision. Approval
capture, catalog admission, hosted promotion, local activation, student use,
speech billing, and progression remain disabled.
