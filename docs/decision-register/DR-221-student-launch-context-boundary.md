# DR-221: Student Launch Context Boundary

Date: 2026-07-15

## Decision

Add a controlled-practice launch context card to direct launch and front-door student routes.

## Rationale

The product needs a consistent safety line on both admin and learner surfaces: demo routes are practice previews until launch gates, school policy, persistence, and report rules are accepted.

## Standard

- Direct launch routes must show `Controlled student practice`.
- Front-door routes must show `Controlled front-door practice`.
- Student-facing demo routes must show `No live classroom launch`, `Target language unlocks progress`, and `No production student accounts`.
- Support language and media remain support-only and cannot unlock progress.
