# DR-1131: Controlled-Pilot Approval Readiness

The release-control decision room now has one review-only contract that joins
composite evidence, pilot review, and the reviewer identity/signature gate.

The contract distinguishes evidence blockers, release-control blockers,
reviewer-gate blockers, and eligibility for a future human review. It records
the required human records and next gate while keeping approval capture,
release mutation, assignment activation, and student launch disabled.
