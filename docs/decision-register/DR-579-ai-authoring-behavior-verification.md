# DR-579: AI Authoring Behavior Verification

Status: Accepted

Decision: Execute the AI authoring service's pedagogical and provider-dispatch safeguards in the compiled foundation behavior harness.

Guardrails:

- Vocabulary counts must remain within 8–12 terms.
- Target sentences must remain exactly two structures.
- Target-language audio and media-rights evidence are required.
- Results remain `review-only` with `providerDispatchAllowed: false`.
- The harness performs no provider call, billing, write, assignment activation, or support-language progression.
