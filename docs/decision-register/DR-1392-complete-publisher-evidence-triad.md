# DR-1392: Complete publisher evidence triad

- **Decision:** Require required rights, accessibility/caption, and scan
  evidence requests in the intake brief and submission manifest.
- **Why:** A structurally complete publisher handoff must not omit a core risk
  lane while still passing inventory and adaptation.
- **Scope:** Intake and manifest validators plus their review-only fixtures.
- **Acceptance:** Each supported evidence kind is required; missing kinds fail
  closed; optional media omission remains explicit; all foundation checks pass.
- **Owner:** Codex architecture and integration review.
- **Next gate:** Human review of the real publisher evidence files.
