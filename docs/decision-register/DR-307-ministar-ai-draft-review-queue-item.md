# DR-307: MiniStar AI Draft Review Queue Item

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-07-31

Decision: Add the MiniStar Level 1 AI-generated greetings draft to the normal teacher draft review queue.

White-label impact: Positive. Generated content follows the same review queue pattern as teacher-created drafts and sample-publisher drafts.

Cost impact: Positive. The queue item is static review evidence and enables no live verifier call, AI generation, voice generation, storage write, approval, route, playlist, local bundle, or assignment.

Constraints:

- English target-language audio remains required before student use.
- Japanese support remains hiragana-only and support-only.
- No verifier submission, package approval, route creation, playlist creation, student assignment, student-ready marker, evidence upload, or live audit transition is enabled.
