# DR-212: Pilot Evidence Packet Preview

Date: 2026-07-14

## Decision

Show a package-level pilot evidence packet preview before enabling live evidence upload or signed approval capture.

## Rationale

Publishers and schools will need a practical checklist of proof before pilot launch. The platform should define the evidence categories now, while keeping the foundation preview blocked from storing real files, signatures, or approvals.

## Standard

- Evidence packet data is derived from the package publish gate and package approval ledger.
- The packet must show gate evidence and approval evidence separately.
- The packet must state `No evidence upload`, `No signed approval capture`, and `Package evidence stays metadata first`.
- The packet cannot approve a pilot, assign students, or override release-control gates.
