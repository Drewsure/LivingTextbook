# DR-180: Draft Audio Coverage Preview

## Decision

Add a draft audio coverage preview to the teacher draft package route.

## Rationale

Every learner-facing text change needs reviewed audio before students use it. The authoring surface should make that requirement visible before live authoring exists.

## Implications

- The draft route shows term, sentence, and instruction audio requirements.
- Audio regeneration remains blocked until a real verifier/audio workflow exists.
- Student assignment remains blocked until edited text has reviewed target-language audio or approved fallback voice coverage.

## Next

Connect draft text edits to audio cue regeneration or human audio review after persistence, voice policy, media rights, and verifier workflow are accepted.
