# DR-040: Publisher Maintenance Contract

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-07-03

## Decision

Treat publisher-owned yearly maintenance of textbook-linked content, music, video, game availability, QR routes, and report policy as a core white-label platform capability.

## Reason

A saleable Living Textbook product must let a publisher maintain its own textbook package over time. This includes annual editions, media updates, optional mid-year refreshes, stable printed QR codes, local/hosted delivery choices, and teacher reporting guardrails.

## White-Label Impact

Positive. This moves the platform beyond a MiniStar-only product and makes it credible for textbook publishers, school networks, and partners who need their own branded multimedia companion.

## Cost Impact

Controlled if handled through shared package manifests and release windows. High if treated as custom manual support for each partner.

## Constraints

- Source content remains partner-owned until reviewed into a package.
- Music and video assets require ownership, rights, version, and delivery metadata.
- Printed QR codes resolve stable platform identifiers, not temporary development routes or direct media files.
- Game availability is config-driven and must use reusable game engines and standard progress events.
- Reports require privacy, retention, role, and export policy before live student records are stored.
- Local and hosted bundles must share manifest vocabulary.

## Follow-Up

Promote the sample maintenance contract into durable record shapes once the backend storage policy is selected.
