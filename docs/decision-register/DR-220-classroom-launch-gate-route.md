# DR-220: Classroom Launch Gate Route

Date: 2026-07-15

## Decision

Add a focused classroom launch gate route for the sample publisher package.

## Rationale

The full teacher intake page is useful for platform operators, but school and publisher review needs a smaller surface that explains why launch remains blocked.

## Standard

- Route: `/teacher/launch-gate/starter-english-level-1-unit-1-2026.1-pilot-candidate-classroom-launch-gate`.
- It must show `No live classroom launch`, `Launch blocked`, `No live student session`, and `No launch button`.
- It may link to source routes for evidence review.
- It cannot create assignments, invite students, store live progress, collect real learner data, export reports, or approve pilot status.
