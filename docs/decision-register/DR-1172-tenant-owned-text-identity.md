# DR-1172: Tenant-Owned Text Identity

Repeated tenant-owned terms, sentences, labels, metrics, reward names, and
quality-check text use deterministic positional or domain-scoped UI identity.
Visible content remains unchanged and duplicate content remains valid. Audio,
reports, scoring, progression, persistence, tenant scope, and review-only
policy are unaffected. Evidence: the affected teacher/student/release
surfaces and `scripts/verify-review-list-key-stability.mjs`.
