# DR-214: Pilot Launch Checklist Preview

Date: 2026-07-15

## Decision

Add a staged pilot launch checklist preview to the teacher/admin intake surface.

## Rationale

The product needs to be saleable without overpromising. A checklist helps explain controlled demo, publisher materials, evidence completion, school policy, technical release, and teacher dry-run requirements before classroom use.

## Standard

- The checklist derives from readiness summary, evidence packet, and pilot handoff package data.
- It must show `No classroom launch action`.
- It must include teacher dry-run requirements.
- It cannot launch students, upload evidence, capture signatures, or mark a package pilot-ready.
