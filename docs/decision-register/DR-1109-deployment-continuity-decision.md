# DR-1109: Deployment Continuity Decision

Decision: Bind hosted PWA, local classroom server, and packaged companion
paths to the shared provider-neutral persistence recovery rehearsal before
selection or activation.

- Expose path-level recovery modes, continuity blockers, and evidence bindings.
- Keep recommendations separate from policy acceptance and provider selection.
- Keep persistence activation, classroom launch, offline-ready status,
  installer export, and route mutation blocked.

References: ADR 1109, Build session 1023, and the content intake verification
checks.
