# DR-052: AI Authoring Verifier Handoff Before Live AI

Date: 2026-07-09

Status: accepted

## Decision

Add an AI authoring verifier handoff scaffold before connecting live AI authoring.

## Rationale

The platform will likely benefit from AI-assisted extraction and curriculum drafting, but the product must not let generated drafts become student-facing content. A visible handoff clarifies which stages are AI-assisted, which are schema/verifier checks, which require rights review, and which require teacher approval.

## Consequences

- AI authoring remains useful but bounded.
- Live AI integration stays deferred.
- Audio coverage, assist-language limits, media rights, and teacher approval are visible from the start.
- Future AI services must obey the existing package workflow.
